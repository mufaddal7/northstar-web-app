ABOUT NORTHSTAR

We build systems businesses actually run on.

Northstar Business Solutions exists because most system implementations solve the wrong problem — they get software installed instead of getting a business to actually run better.

THE FOUNDER

Started by someone who watched it go wrong first

Northstar was founded by Burhanuddin Motiwala after years spent close to manufacturing and trading businesses, watching the same pattern repeat: a business invests in an ERP or a dashboard, the go-live happens on schedule, and within months the team is quietly back to spreadsheets and WhatsApp groups.

The software was rarely the problem. The gap was almost always the same — nobody had mapped how the business actually worked before configuring the system to run it. Northstar was built to close that gap directly, and to stay in the business long enough to prove the system actually gets used, not just delivered.

WHAT MAKES US DIFFERENT

Three things we do that most implementation partners don't

01

We map the workflow before we touch the software

Process and workflow mapping happens first, every time — how approvals move, where decisions get made, where information actually lives today. The system gets configured to that reality, not the other way around.
02

We stay until adoption is complete, not until go-live

Go-live is the midpoint of our engagement, not the finish line. We train every user, sit with the team through the first real reporting cycles, and only step back once the system is the default way work gets done.
03

We combine systems work with the advisory layer

ERP, BI, and digital platforms are tools. We pair them with strategic and digital transformation advisory so the systems we build are actually pointed at the business outcomes that matter.
