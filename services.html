WHAT WE DO

Ten capabilities. One partner.

From the ERP running your operations to the website bringing in your next customer — Northstar covers the full stack of systems a growing business needs, grouped into four practices.

01 · SYSTEMS & OPERATIONS

The engine your business runs on

ERP Implementation & Optimization

CORE

End-to-end ERP selection, implementation, and optimization — configured around your actual workflow, not a generic template. We handle data migration, integrations, and the tuning that happens after go-live, so the system keeps improving instead of quietly breaking.

Process & Workflow Automation

We identify the manual, repeated, error-prone steps in your operations and automate them — inside your ERP, across connected tools, or with purpose-built scripts — so work moves without waiting on someone to push it forward.

Change Management & User Adoption

Structured training, role-based onboarding, and hands-on support through the first full reporting cycles — the layer most implementations skip, and the reason most systems get half-used.

02 · DATA & INTELLIGENCE

Decisions backed by evidence, not instinct

Business Intelligence Dashboards

CORE

Live dashboards built on clean, agreed-upon definitions — so the numbers on screen are numbers your leadership team actually trusts and acts on, not a prettier version of the same confusion.

Data Analytics & Reporting

We consolidate data scattered across spreadsheets, ERP modules, and point tools into structured reporting that answers the specific questions your business needs answered, on the schedule you actually need them.

AI Automation

Targeted AI automation for the repetitive analysis, drafting, and data-processing work that eats up hours — built on top of clean data and integrated into the systems your team already uses.

03 · DIGITAL PRESENCE & GROWTH

Where new business finds you

Web Solutions

Websites and web platforms built to represent the business accurately and convert visitors into enquiries — fast, mobile-ready, and built on infrastructure that won't need replacing in a year.

SEO Optimization

Technical and content SEO that gets your business found by the people already searching for what you do — structured for long-term ranking, not short-term tricks that fade.

04 · ADVISORY & INFRASTRUCTURE

The strategy layer behind the systems

Strategic Advisory

Direct, practitioner-led advisory on operations, growth, and organizational structure — for leadership teams who want a second set of eyes that's actually been inside businesses like theirs.

Digital Transformation Strategy

UMBRELLA

A single roadmap connecting your systems, data, and digital presence — so every project we run for you moves the same business forward, instead of solving problems in isolation.

Cloud & IT Infrastructure Advisory

Guidance on the infrastructure underneath everything else — cloud hosting, security basics, backups, and access control — sized correctly for a growing business, not oversold.
