ABOUT NORTHSTAR

Systems mapped to the business, not the other way around.

Northstar is a digital transformation partner for growing businesses — building the ERP, data, and digital infrastructure leadership teams rely on to make decisions with confidence.

THE FOUNDER

Burhanuddin Motiwala

Northstar was founded on a simple observation: most businesses don't struggle because they lack the right software. They struggle because the software was never built around how they actually work.

Burhanuddin Motiwala founded Northstar to close that gap — senior-level ERP, data, and digital advisory that starts with how a business actually operates, not with a product demo.

WHAT MAKES US DIFFERENT

A short list of things we hold ourselves to.

  • We map the business before we touch any software
  • We stay senior, end to end — no handoff to a junior delivery team
  • We measure success in adoption, not just go-live
  • We work as one accountable partner, not five disconnected vendors
